# User Guide

Welcome to the **AI-Powered Enterprise Pre-Sales Command Center**. This guide will help you navigate the system.

## Navigation
- **Global Search:** Use `Ctrl/Cmd+K` from anywhere in the app to search across Opportunities, Accounts, and Tasks.
- **Left Sidebar:** Access your primary modules: Dashboard, Opportunities, Accounts, and Bids.

## Opportunities
1. **List View:** View your active pipeline in the data grid.
2. **Detail View:** Manage the deal through the Lifecycle Stepper. 
3. **AI Copilot Insights:** On every deal, the right panel will surface 'Business Insights', such as Win Prediction probability and recommended actions.

## Tasks & Kanban
- Navigate to `/tasks` to drag and drop tasks across stages. The system automatically assigns AI-suggested priorities based on CRM data.
